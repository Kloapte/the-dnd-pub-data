# Core Rules Foundation Phase 5 Rule Gap Prioritization

## Scope and decision status

This report evaluates all 46 gap families in the completed Rule semantic inventory. It is an analysis and recommendation artifact only. No schema, canonical record, conversion disposition, validator, or Java code is changed here.

The established baseline is 231 semantically reviewed Rule records: 60 SUPPORTED, 156 PARTIAL, and 15 UNSUPPORTED. These are contract-fit classifications, not conversion coverage. Every Rule remains eligible for a stable canonical identity; a Rule with no represented mechanics is reference-only and is not thereby FULL.

The importance, reusability, complexity, and recommendation columns are new Codex judgments for human review. Exact affected source identities and JSONPaths are maintained in the [Rule gap inventory](CORE_RULES_FOUNDATION_PHASE_5_RULE_GAP_INVENTORY.md); counts below are distinct records and can overlap across families.

## Recommended order for IMPLEMENT_NOW

These are recommendations for a later, separately authorized Rule contract refinement. They are not approval to implement.

1. **Finite D20 inputs and formulas — R-GAP-01, R-GAP-05, R-GAP-06, R-GAP-10, R-GAP-11.** Define the ability-score modifier, DC forms, proficiency application/scaling, passive-score formula, and ability/Skill options as separate bounded values. These are connected inputs but not one generic formula language. Start with the source records XPHB Ability Score and Modifier, Difficulty Class, Proficiency, Passive Perception, PHB Skills with Different Abilities, and DMG Skill Variants.
2. **Core resolution qualifiers — R-GAP-04 and R-GAP-09.** Add Player Character applicability for Death Saving Throws and distinguish Initiative Score/side initiative from speed-factor variants. Do not include encounter scheduling or merge all initiative variants.
3. **Damage and recovery procedures — R-GAP-20 and R-GAP-18.** Add damage-type resistance/vulnerability as a single damage-instance adjustment; then extend Rest for the source-defined interruption and benefit branches while preserving edition differences.
4. **Falls and spatial geometry — R-GAP-15, R-GAP-36, and R-GAP-45.** Represent fall cadence and flight interaction, area origin/line-of-effect behavior, and grid-distance procedures separately. These can share spatial references but must not share a generic spatial engine.

## Omission shortlist for later human review

The following OMIT_FOR_NOW recommendations are ordered by potential value for later review. They are not permanent exclusions. Deferred families such as R-GAP-13 and R-GAP-28 remain assigned to later-domain dependencies rather than this omission shortlist.

1. **R-GAP-24 — Madness outcomes and recovery.** Two affected Rules, with existing Condition structures potentially reusable, but the table mixes conditions, durations, restrictions, and recovery. Revisit only if a bounded source-selected consequence model can use typed Condition/Spell references without becoming a general effects table.
2. **R-GAP-23 — Lingering injuries.** The DMG table has broad gameplay impact but heterogeneous long-term consequences. Revisit after Condition, Equipment, healing, and character state identities can be referenced.
3. **R-GAP-32 — Planar effects.** Broad consequences, but one optional record and many effect domains; revisit only with its own source-bounded family inventory.
4. **R-GAP-42 and R-GAP-44 — Massive damage and morale.** Both are optional encounter procedures. Consider separately only if later encounter patterns establish reusable bounded threshold/check outcomes; do not merge their consequences.
5. **R-GAP-22, R-GAP-27, and R-GAP-43 — Prophet's Gambit, Emrakul corruption, and potion mixtures.** Each is a source-specific subsystem or outcome with little cross-record reuse.
6. **R-GAP-19 and R-GAP-40 — Knot-derived DC and cleaving.** Both have clear rules, but each is one isolated procedure with modest benefit relative to additional contract surface.

## Cross-gap reuse opportunities and boundaries

- **Numerical D20 inputs:** R-GAP-01 and R-GAP-06 provide values reused by R-GAP-05, R-GAP-09, R-GAP-10, and R-GAP-11. Keep ability modifier, proficiency, DC, passive score, and skill/ability selection as distinct typed values.
- **Damage and objects:** R-GAP-20 can supply a bounded damage-type adjustment used by future R-GAP-08 and R-GAP-39 procedures. Damage mitigation is not object AC/HP or cover redirection.
- **Movement and space:** R-GAP-15, R-GAP-21, R-GAP-36, R-GAP-41, and R-GAP-45 all need spatial/Speed references, but falling cadence, teleportation, affected-area geometry, creature speed modes, and grid measurement remain separate procedures.
- **Rest and resources:** R-GAP-18's rest events could later be referenced by R-GAP-13, R-GAP-16, R-GAP-25, and R-GAP-46. Rest completion must not itself define every resource refresh.
- **Inspiration and Hero Points:** R-GAP-25 and R-GAP-46 share a character-held resource concern, but their award, pool, timing, and outcome rules differ. Prefer a small Character Building resource identity if that domain supports it; retain Rule-specific spend procedures independently.
- **Proficiency and character progression:** R-GAP-06 may share a bounded proficiency-bonus value with R-GAP-30, R-GAP-34, R-GAP-37, and R-GAP-38. Do not make class/sidekick progression a Rule-owned proficiency engine.

## Confidence limits and human decisions

- The family counts and examples are taken from the completed semantic inventory. R-GAP-18 is 8 records in that inventory (including Planar Effects), R-GAP-06 is 6, and R-GAP-31 is 12; earlier prose in the historical gap report may contain older counts.
- R-GAP-13 is particularly broad: its 38 distinct records do not share one procedure. Before implementation, human review should decide whether to address selected activity families incrementally or leave them reference-only until dependent domains exist.
- R-GAP-09 combines a straightforward Initiative Score with materially more complex side and speed-factor variants. Human review should approve the precise subset if it is selected.
- R-GAP-36 combines XPHB area/origin rules with an XGE Spellcasting grid procedure. Human review should confirm the Rule/Spell boundary and whether the grid-specific portion belongs in Rule or World Building.
- R-GAP-41 and R-GAP-31 are family labels over mechanically distinct movement and maritime procedures. Their record-level association is useful for prioritization but does not authorize one shared implementation.
- DM-selected DCs, optional rules, and adjudicated outcomes are source choices, not semantic ambiguity. Where the source leaves an outcome to the DM, a future contract should preserve that discretion rather than infer a value.

The present inventory supports prioritization but not complete future-domain contracts or runtime semantics. No recommendation changes the 231 discovery statuses, the 28-record canonical conversion checkpoint, or the Phase 5 approval state.

## Established decisions and new recommendations

The architectural requirements that every Rule be referenceable, that reference-only records are valid, and that mechanical coverage remain separate are established project decisions. The 46 ratings, recommendations, implementation order, and omission shortlist above are new recommendations only and require human review. Phase 5 remains open and unapproved.
