## Conversion and review conventions

- Preserve each source-qualified identity as `item:<lowercase-source>:<slugged-name>`; preserve distinct source/edition records. Never merge reprints based on name alone.
- Treat base-item definitions and item catalog entries as one Item model, per the approved schema. Expand only source-defined ordinary templates when the source evidence and approved contract support the expansion. Do not interpret `magicvariants.json` or generic magic-item inheritance here.
- Apply source copy/override content in source order: retain the copy identity and source metadata, inherit only what the source explicitly carries through, and treat local override fields as authoritative. Missing inherited/overridden values are not invented.
- Normalize structured source facts first. Convert price, weight, category, weapon/armor values, range, capacity, and properties into their approved typed fields. Preserve PHB/XPHB distinctions and exact source codes.
- Interpret prose only into approved typed mechanics. Keep activation, resolution, delivery, and outcomes distinct where the schema does. Reuse Step 2 action, check, save, damage, condition, and reference contracts only when their meanings match. Do not simulate play or add untyped prose fallbacks.
- Resolve a reference only when the source identifies a target adequately. Preserve unresolved target intent and exact source locator in external accounting; do not infer equivalence from a shared display name.
- Account for each meaningful source portion as interpreted, duplicate, omitted, ambiguous, or deferred. Omission/duplicate registers must use exact source locators and reasons and must not reproduce source entries.
- Keep conversion decisions authored and reviewed by Codex. Scripts may inventory/extract source records and validate IDs, schemas, references, and coverage; they may not assign scope or interpret mechanics.
