## Rules for future associations

Retain document/section/row/option boundaries before trying to add meaning. A name/source qualifier is authored reference evidence; it is not a targetId unless the existing resolver already supplies an authoritative result. Do not introduce shared source defaults, selected choices, calculated quantities, effect recipients or implicit trigger/duration defaults.

Explicit label sequences differ from relationships. There are 529 actSave, 548 actSaveFail, 252 actSaveSuccess and 84 actSaveSuccessOrFail occurrences in the full earlier corpus inventory, including support/transform contexts. Their unequal counts alone defeat an unconditional one-save/one-failure/one-success matching algorithm. Even equal counts would not prove a relation.
