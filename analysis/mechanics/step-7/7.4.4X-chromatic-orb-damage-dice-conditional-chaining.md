## Typed interpretation

Three revision-1 claims describe one initial attack, reusable damage, and the conditional chain. No
claim is generated per leap or target.

The attack claim is one cast-time `RANGED_SPELL` attack by `CASTER`. Its target scope is
`SINGLE_TARGET_UNSPECIFIED_CATEGORY`, preserving the source's “target” wording. It has one attack
roll, no count progression, and no automatic HIT. The source's 90-foot range cannot be represented
in the attack payload, so the attack claim is PARTIAL with an explicit diagnostic.

The separate damage claim is `3d8`, with one choice among exactly acid, cold, fire, lightning,
poison, and thunder. The type is chosen before the attack and remains the orb's type through its
leaps. Damage is bound to HIT on the exact initial attack. Slot levels 1–9 produce 3d8 through 11d8
by adding 1d8 per level above 1st. Damage has no modifier.

The chain references the initial attack and reusable damage by exact ID, revision, and content hash.
Its trigger compares individual d8 faces in one Chromatic Orb damage roll: at least two equal faces
enable one leap. It does not compare damage totals, attack d20 results, maximum damage, or critical
status. Each new target requires an attack roll and a new damage roll, and the trigger can be
reevaluated on that roll. The reference graph is acyclic: damage depends on the initial attack; the
chain depends on the initial attack and damage.

Each leap targets a different target within 30 feet of the previous target. A creature may be
targeted only once per casting. Since the source does not delimit every use of “target” to creatures,
the model leaves target category unspecified while retaining the explicit creature reuse rule. No
visibility requirement is added.

At slot level `L` from 1 through 9, at most `L` conditional leaps can occur if each trigger is met.
The source's restriction that the orb cannot leap again unless cast with a level-2-or-higher slot
means a first-level casting permits at most one leap; higher slots permit the additional leaps up to
their slot level. These are maximums, not guaranteed attacks: slot 1 allows at most the initial
attack plus one leap; slot 2 allows at most the initial attack plus two leaps; slot 9 allows at most
the initial attack plus nine leaps. Damage dice scaling and leap count are independent.

The source does not state whether critical-hit or external damage dice count toward the match, or
what a missed later attack does to the chain. Those behaviors remain unspecified and have explicit
diagnostics; no outcome is invented.
