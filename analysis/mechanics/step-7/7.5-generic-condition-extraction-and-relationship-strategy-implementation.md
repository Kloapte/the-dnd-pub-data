## Implementation

`GenericConditionExtractionStrategy` recognizes authored `APPLY`, `REMOVE`, `GRANT_IMMUNITY`, and `PREVENT` operations. Explicit mutually exclusive alternatives use `ConditionChoiceMechanic`; alternatives are not emitted as simultaneous effects. Condition-tag-only candidates produce `CONDITION_TAG_SEMANTICS_UNRESOLVED`.

`GenericConditionDurationExtractionStrategy` emits a separate duration mechanic only when the duration occurs in the same authored clause as the condition reference. It supports explicit fixed durations, next-turn boundaries, and a uniquely referenced declared spell duration. A spell-wide duration in a separate clause is not automatically attached to a condition.

`ConditionRelationshipStrategy` resolves explicit initial/repeated save outcomes, condition durations, spell-duration termination, and successful action/check termination. Action-based ending uses a separate typed `ConditionTerminationEventMechanic`; the relationship points from the condition to that event. The event model records the eligible actor role and an explicitly named ability-check ability where present. These are declarative facts, not an event executor.

Relationship strategies are enumerated once by stable strategy ID/version even when a strategy supports multiple relationship types. This prevents duplicate execution and duplicate relationship IDs.

The canonical PHB condition extractor retains its pre-existing condition vocabulary. The new `EXHAUSTION` enum value is available to the experimental path, while a compatibility guard preserves the previous canonical treatment until a separate canonical migration is authorized.

## Diagnostics and remaining limits

Condition diagnostics remain visible rather than being upgraded to claims. Counts include 327 ambiguous save/condition pairings, 254 unresolved structured condition tags, 183 unresolved operation mentions, 71 condition-duration associations that could not be established, 28 unresolved condition names, and 27 unresolved choices. The complete diagnostic inventory is in the JSON artifact. The four extracted action-ending events were uniquely linked; source patterns outside the bounded action/check grammar remain unrecognized or unresolved rather than inferred.

The condition payload's typed vocabulary is bounded. It does not model arbitrary duration clocks, all condition-ending external events, multiple simultaneous selections, or a general condition state machine. Conditions embedded in complex exclusions, nested rules, and broad authored paragraphs can remain unresolved. A recognized condition is not evidence that every associated save, duration, or termination rule has been interpreted.

The spell-wide coverage scope remains `RECOGNIZED_FRAGMENTS_ONLY`; no owner is promoted to complete by this run. Human review and licensing remain independent and pending.
