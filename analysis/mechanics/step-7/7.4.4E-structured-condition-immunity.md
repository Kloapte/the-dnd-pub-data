## Method and rule boundary

Each of the 12 discovery candidates was checked against its structured condition list, authored
Spell prose, target, and duration in its pinned source record. The implementation accepts a
condition only when its structured tag is corroborated by direct immunity wording and the source
identifies the affected creature and a timed spell duration. Conditions are emitted separately as
`ConditionChangeMechanic(GRANT_IMMUNITY, ...)`, with stable owner-and-condition slots.

The rule reuses `PhbConditionMechanicsRules`, `SpellMechanicsEvidenceSource`, and
`SpellMechanicsAssembly`. Claims associate the relevant `/conditionImmune/{index}` field, exact
prose phrase, touched/willing target phrase, and declared duration field. The declared-duration
reference includes the verified source revision. Concentration is bound when the source duration
requires it. Conditional wording such as “while in this form” remains a claim qualifier.

An immunity tag does not itself imply that the spell removes an existing condition or prevents
another effect from imposing one. Those meanings are only emitted when directly supported by
separate source prose and the current model can represent them. No `REMOVE` claims are inferred
from `conditionImmune`.
