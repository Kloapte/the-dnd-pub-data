## Finite enchanted stones and lifetime

`EnchantedObjectCollectionMechanic` describes one source-defined finite collection of pebbles, with
maximum count three. It records that the spell enchants objects already touched rather than creating
them, that the enchantment is applied with the bonus-action casting at touch range, and that each
stone's enchantment ends after that stone is used for an attack regardless of outcome. Consumption
applies only to the used stone. Transferring or holding an unused stone is not modeled as a use.
The mechanic does not create runtime stone identities, counters, replenishment, or a promised number
of attack rolls; it does not say that the physical pebble is destroyed.

The enchantment duration references the spell's declared one-minute duration. The source does not
require concentration. Its separate recasting sentence ends any still-active enchantments from the
previous casting; that spell-wide replacement rule does not consume the other stones when one stone
is used. The source does not restrict the pebbles to nonmagical objects, so no such eligibility
restriction is asserted.
