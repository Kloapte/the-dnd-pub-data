# Step 7.4.3C — Spell Modifiers, Resistances and Protective Effects (partial)

## Scope and result

This bounded PHB batch adds eight previously unprocessed owners and eleven proposed mechanics.
The catalog now processes 95 of 969 Spell owners with 171 typed mechanics. The pinned PHB records
were inspected before selecting patterns. No pinned source JSON or benchmark fixture changed.

New canonical types are `StatisticModifierMechanic`, `ModifierOperation`, and `ModifiedStatistic`
for additive, subtractive, multiplicative, replacement, base replacement, and minimum operations; `DamageResistanceMechanic`,
`DamageProtectionOperation`, and `DamageApplicability` distinguish resistance from immunity and
retain the nonmagical-only Stoneskin restriction. Existing `ArmorClassModifierMechanic` represents
Shield of Faith's additive AC bonus, `AmountExpression` represents fixed and d4 values, and the
existing `DamageTypeSelection` represents Protection from Energy's one-of-five choice. Shield's
`SpellDamagePreventionMechanic` remains unchanged.

`AppliesWhen` records willing-target, armor-wearing, and roll-before-spell-ends qualifiers.
`ModifierOnSaveOutcome` links Bane's penalty to its failed Charisma save requirement. The existing
duration and concentration bindings are attached to their relevant claims. `SpellTargetSubject`
preserves a maximum target count for Bless and Bane.

| PHB Spell | Claims | Structured interpretation |

| Shield of Faith | 1 | +2 AC, single selected creature, duration and concentration |
| Mage Armor | 1 | Base AC becomes 13 + Dexterity modifier; willing and unarmored target; partial ending rules |

Claims preserve stable owner/slot IDs, exact source evidence spans or field pointers, source revisions,
and deterministic extraction. New claims remain PENDING_REVIEW with NEEDS_REVIEW licensing. No
publication decision was changed. The default public REST response is unchanged; the mechanics remain
available only through the established private inspection opt-in.

## Candidate decisions and remaining limitations

- Shield is unchanged and remains the regression case for additive AC and Magic Missile damage
  prevention.
- Absorb Elements is absent from `spells-phb.json`; it was not extracted from another edition.
- The PHB candidate set has no matching condition that supports a new damage-immunity claim.
  The canonical protection operation distinguishes immunity from resistance, while no immunity
  owner was added.
- Mage Armor's exact source evidence is preserved, but the rule ending the spell when armor is donned
  or when dismissed as an action remains untyped and the base-AC claim is PARTIAL.
- No new generic numeric damage-reduction pattern was supported by these candidates. Existing Shield
  prevention remains a distinct, source-backed spell-specific mechanic.
- The modifier, resistance and protection patterns cover only the eight named PHB owners. Other
  modifiers, stacking rules, conditional protections, damage immunities and ending conditions remain
  unattempted or unsupported. No stacking behavior is inferred.
- All six benchmark fixtures remain candidate diagnostics pending human review; this batch did not
  modify expected fixtures or claim benchmark acceptance.
