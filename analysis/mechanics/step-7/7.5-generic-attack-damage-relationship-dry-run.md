## Limitations

- An attack is emitted only when its attack kind and singular/group count are supported by the local authored fragment.
- Attacks expressed once per ray or target across sibling entries remain unmodeled until shared source context is resolved generically.
- Conditional chains and delayed timing are not executed; delayed damage keeps an explicit timing diagnostic.
- Relationship evidence identifies source fragments and exact source revisions; current evidence spans cover the normalized authored fragment.
- Every candidate remains experimental, PARTIAL, PENDING_REVIEW, NEEDS_REVIEW for licensing, and unauthorized for publication.
