## Remaining limitations

- Remaining `UNSUPPORTED`: 428; remaining `NOT_ASSESSED`: 381.
- The canonical assembly marks coverage `PARTIAL` whenever it emits a claim and always leaves `/entries` unassessed. This prevents inventory records from reaching `SUPPORTED` even when every interpreted clause in the assessed scope is supported. Coverage tracking was not redesigned in this batch.
- Other riders, repeated actions, conditional triggers and higher-level scaling that cannot be bound unambiguously remain outside the new save and damage claims. Source prose outside the recognized local clauses remains unassessed.
- Evidence-quality check: the preserved/current owner identity sets are equal; baseline unsupported reason keys: 10 baseline, 15 current.
- Most frequent remaining unsupported reasons: SAVING_THROW_REQUIREMENT:SOURCE_CLASSIFICATION_WITHOUT_MATCHING_PATTERN=272, DAMAGE:SOURCE_CLASSIFICATION_WITHOUT_MATCHING_PATTERN=252, CONDITION:SOURCE_CLASSIFICATION_WITHOUT_MATCHING_PATTERN=131, ATTACK_ROLL:SOURCE_CLASSIFICATION_WITHOUT_MATCHING_PATTERN=42, SPELL_SLOT_SCALING:NO_SUPPORTED_PATTERN=35.
- Most frequent unassessed reasons: UNASSESSED_SOURCE_CONTENT:/entries=969, UNASSESSED_SOURCE_CONTENT:/entriesHigherLevel=967, NO_MATCHING_PATTERN_SOURCE_CONTENT_UNASSESSED=766.

## Reusable structures assessed

1. `must make` / `must succeed on` ability save clauses, with locally stated subjects.
2. Explicit failed-save damage and successful-save half/no-damage clauses, including outcome text adjacent to the damage sentence.
3. Multiple damage tags with separately stated damage types and shared save outcomes.
4. Simple dice and dice-plus-integer amounts, plus existing single-damage slot scaling.
5. Unsupported ambiguous multi-component scaling, repeated saves, and conditional riders remain explicit gaps.

## Next extraction families by observed impact

Saving throws and damage had the largest observed structured gaps in the 7.4.4A inventory, so this batch addressed those first. The next largest current gaps are condition (131 owners), attack roll (42), spell-slot scaling (35 unsupported pattern diagnostics), and damage resistance (33 owners). Condition and damage-related gaps are aggregated separately; categories may overlap on a Spell.

The broad offline pass yielded claims for representative save-only owners (Calm Emotions), single damage plus save owners (Acid Splash, Arms of Hadar, Call Lightning, Disintegrate, Dissonant Whispers, Thunderwave), and multi-component damage (Ice Storm). Only those eight owners were added to bounded production matching.
