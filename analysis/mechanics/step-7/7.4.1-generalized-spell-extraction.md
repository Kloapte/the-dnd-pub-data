# Step 7.4.1 — Generalized Spell Mechanics Extraction

## Scope and result

This batch adds a small ordered pattern registry to the startup Spell extraction path. It retains the
Shield extractor as an edition-specific adapter and composes it with bounded PHB damage/save and
healing patterns. The existing PHB Fireball and Healing Word outputs remain compatible; new owners
use stable pattern IDs and source-specific evidence IDs. Source resources are read once per unique
resource during startup extraction and are never scanned by HTTP requests.

The owner set remains explicit. A registered owner is processed with its source evidence; a wording
or schema mismatch produces an abstention/unsupported diagnostic rather than a claim. Integrity
failures such as owner or fingerprint mismatches remain startup failures. Registry ordering is
deterministic, overlapping owner applicability is rejected, and the resulting records and evidence
collections remain immutable.

## Reusable patterns

| Pattern | Supported meaning | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `save-requirement-v1` | One explicitly authored saving throw with a source-listed ability and no invented DC | Requires one save ability in the normalized source field and one matching save phrase in the same bounded entry |
| `damage-on-failed-save-v1` | One tagged dice formula and one explicit damage type, linked to the sole explicitly authored save on failure | Formula must be a single `damage` tag; source field must contain exactly one damage type; failed-save wording must occur in the same sentence; success outcomes remain unsupported |
| `healing-amount-v1` | One `dice` formula plus the authored spellcasting ability modifier as an ordered sum | Requires explicit “hit points equal to” wording and the exact symbolic modifier phrase; target and eligibility rules remain outside the payload |

Damage claims retain an unknown subject and an explicit `AppliesOnSaveOutcome(FAILURE)` binding to
the saving throw record. Healing claims retain the dice term and symbolic modifier in source order.
All new claims are partial. Each carries authored text and/or structured-field evidence with the
source resource fingerprint, owner pointer, JSON Pointer, UTF-16 span where textual, and pattern
revision. They do not create executable effects or resolved cross-catalog references.

The recognized half-damage wording is recorded as unsupported. Area, targeting, ongoing damage,
eligibility, and scaling remain unassessed unless independently represented by a bounded diagnostic.
No extraction declares a complete Spell interpretation.

## Candidate screen and selected owners

`candidate-screen.mjs` parses each pinned Spell JSON resource and recursively inspects entry values.
Its broad screen requires a `damage`, `dice`, `scaledamage`, or `scaledice` tag plus either structured
`damageInflict`/`savingThrow` metadata or “regains hit points” wording. This produced 372 broad
candidates. The screen is only a discovery aid; its count is not an extraction-eligibility or
accuracy measure. Nine additional PHB owners passed manual bounded-pattern review and tests:

| Spell ID | Source pointer | Pattern | Reason selected |
|---|---|---|---|
| `spell:phb:burning-hands` | `/spell/40` | Damage/save | One damage formula, one explicit Dexterity save, one damage type, failed-save clause |
| `spell:phb:circle-of-death` | `/spell/47` | Damage/save | One formula/type and a single explicit Constitution-save/failure relationship |
| `spell:phb:cone-of-cold` | `/spell/60` | Damage/save | One formula/type and a single explicit Constitution-save/failure relationship |
| `spell:phb:cure-wounds` | `/spell/84` | Healing | Explicit healing dice plus the symbolic spellcasting modifier |
| `spell:phb:lightning-bolt` | `/spell/204` | Damage/save | One formula/type and a single explicit Dexterity-save/failure relationship |
| `spell:phb:mass-cure-wounds` | `/spell/217` | Healing | Explicit healing dice plus the symbolic spellcasting modifier |
| `spell:phb:mass-healing-word` | `/spell/219` | Healing | Explicit healing dice plus the symbolic spellcasting modifier |
| `spell:phb:prayer-of-healing` | `/spell/259` | Healing | Explicit healing dice plus the symbolic spellcasting modifier |
| `spell:phb:shatter` | `/spell/293` | Damage/save | One formula/type and a single explicit Constitution-save/failure relationship |

The 372 candidates also include multi-formula, multi-save, attack-gated, conditional, repeated-turn,
choice-dependent and otherwise context-heavy entries. They were not selected merely because a formula
tag or mechanics keyword occurred. For example, Acid Splash needs multiple-target/level alternatives;
Arms of Hadar combines damage with an additional reaction restriction; Ray of Sickness gates damage
on an attack and then applies a condition on a save; Phantasmal Force has conditional ongoing
damage; and Disintegrate uses a dice-plus-flat-value expression with additional hit-point and
transformation consequences. These examples require relationships or payloads outside this batch.
Magic Missile and Prestidigitation remain unattempted.

For selected owners, body text is read only from `/entries/0`; saving throw and damage type checks
come from `/savingThrow/0` and `/damageInflict/0` respectively. Healing patterns require the single
text body and a tagged `dice` formula in that same body. The scanner records each authored root
pointer; it does not copy source prose into its output.

## Limitations and handoff to 7.4.2

The patterns require an authored owner in the explicit reviewed set. The initial broad screen is not
an automatic corpus-wide extractor. Successful-save half damage is not represented as a Damage
mechanic; damage remains a partial failed-save claim with an unsupported success outcome. The
canonical payload does not currently express full target/area constraints, creature exclusions,
object ignition, or higher-level scaling for these records. Healing subjects remain unknown, so
multi-target selection is not inferred. Formula observations remain separate from mechanics.

Step 7.4.2 should begin with adjudicating the newly added source-bounded cases and deciding the next
small pattern scope from explicit unsupported families. It must not broaden into general scaling,
conditions, resistances or arbitrary Spell prose interpretation without separate evidence and
model coverage. Benchmark fixture approval remains a separate human-review action.
