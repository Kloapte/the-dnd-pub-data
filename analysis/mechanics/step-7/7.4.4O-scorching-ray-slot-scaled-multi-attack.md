## Model and extraction

Added `SpellSlotInstanceCount` as an `InstanceCountProgression` variant. It stores the base spell
level, base total instance count, and instances added per slot level. Its total-count calculation
rejects slot levels below the base spell level or above 9 and detects integer overflow. The model
requires positive base counts and increments; `AttackRollMechanic` requires its `attackCount` to
match the progression's base count and requires independent attacks. The existing character-level
progression used by Eldritch Blast is unchanged.

Added `TargetCategoryEligibilityStatus` to `InstanceTargetAllocation`. A non-empty category list
continues to mean source-supported eligible categories. An omitted category list is permitted only
with a typed status such as `UNSPECIFIED_IN_SOURCE` or `NOT_ASSESSED`; an empty list never means
unrestricted targeting. Existing allocations created through the four-argument constructor keep the
same JSON shape when their status is absent. Document validation requires a source-unspecified
allocation to use `UnknownSubject(NOT_AUTHORED)`; it does not treat an unknown subject as a positive
target category.

Scorching Ray's attack claim represents `RANGED_SPELL`, actor `CASTER`, three base independent
attacks, and cast-time activation. Every ray selects exactly one target within range; target reuse is
allowed. `eligibleTargetCategories` is omitted and `targetCategoryEligibilityStatus` is
`UNSPECIFIED_IN_SOURCE`. The attack claim has no reverse effect bindings.

The separate damage claim carries 2d6 fire damage per successful ray. Its
`AppliesOnEachAttackRollOutcome(HIT, CORRESPONDING_ATTACK_INSTANCE)` binding points to the attack
claim's exact revision and content hash. A missed ray therefore has no damage event; a hit on one ray
does not trigger damage for any other ray. No combat simulator or per-ray claim IDs were added.

The source-backed diagnostic `scorching-ray-target-category-unspecified` uses reason
