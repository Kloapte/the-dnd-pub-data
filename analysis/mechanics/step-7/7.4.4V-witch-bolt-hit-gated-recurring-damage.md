## Attack and persistent-link design

The attack claim represents one `RANGED_SPELL` attack by `CASTER` at `CAST_TIME` against one
creature. It has no repeated attack activation, fixed attack progression, or automatic HIT.

`PersistentSpellEffectMechanic` now supports optional typed establishment and termination fields.
For Witch Bolt, the persistent link references the exact attack claim revision and hash with an
explicit `HIT` establishment outcome. A MISS therefore establishes no link. The effect references
the same target subject, requires concentration, references the declared spell duration, is not an
independently acting creature, and only becomes usable through an activation. Duration does not
imply any number of damage applications.

The link records these typed termination conditions: concentration ends; duration expires; the
target moves outside spell range from the caster; the target has total cover from the caster; or the
caster uses a subsequent action for another purpose. Partial cover is not included. Recasting
behavior is unspecified in this source, so `endsWhenSpellIsCastAgain` is omitted instead of asserting
either outcome.

The current attack model cannot encode the initial attack's “within range” qualifier or its 30-foot
value. `witch-bolt-unsupported:initial-attack-range` records this as
`ATTACK_ROLL:FAMILY_NOT_IMPLEMENTED` with evidence from both the authored target phrase and the
structured range. The casting range is not silently substituted into the attack payload. The attack
claim and owner coverage are therefore PARTIAL.

## Damage and activation

Initial damage is `1d12` lightning and is bound to HIT on the exact initial attack claim. MISS has no
damage binding. `SpellSlotDamageScaling(1d12, base 1, minimum 2, interval 1)` applies only to this
initial damage: slots 1 through 9 deal `1d12` through `9d12` respectively. It does not scale the
spell's later action damage.

Subsequent damage is a separate `1d12` lightning `DamageMechanic`. Its typed `ActionActivatedDamage`
requires an optional action by the spell caster on a subsequent turn, references the HIT-established
link, and names the established link target. The damage resolves automatically after that deliberate
activation without another attack roll or saving throw. It has no turn-boundary timing, no attack
reference, no slot scaling, and no fixed total number of applications. It cannot be activated through
this claim after the referenced link is no longer active; runtime enforcement is out of scope.

The typed dependency graph is:

```text
initial attack ──► HIT-established persistent link ──► subsequent action damage
       └────────────────────────────────────────────► initial HIT damage
```

The attack has no dependency on the link. Every cross-claim reference is same-owner and binds the
exact revision and content hash; document validation checks the referenced mechanic type and target.
