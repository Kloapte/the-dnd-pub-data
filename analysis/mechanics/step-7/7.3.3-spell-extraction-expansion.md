### Components

- [`PhbSpellMechanicsExtractor`](../../../src/main/java/com/thedndpub/data/mechanics/spell/PhbSpellMechanicsExtractor.java)
  recognizes three explicitly bounded patterns over normalized entries and verified original source.
- [`SpellMechanicsExtractionService`](../../../src/main/java/com/thedndpub/data/mechanics/spell/SpellMechanicsExtractionService.java)
  selects exactly four owners at startup, verifies pinned inputs, and preserves the other documents.
- The former Shield-local assembler and controlled mismatch exception are renamed to
  `SpellMechanicsAssembly` and `SpellPatternMismatch`. The assembler adds explicit partial-claim
  construction, unsupported concepts and configurable adapter/scope metadata. Existing Shield
  derivation, ordering, evidence IDs and output remain unchanged.
- `SpellMechanicsEvidenceSource`, the Batch 5 balanced parser, existing annotation references,
  canonical validators and inspection mapper are reused. No production class reads benchmark files
  or calls `BenchmarkTestDocuments.materialize`.

There is no language-understanding framework, saving-throw executor, damage calculation, healing
calculation or slot-scaling engine. Formula dice counts/sides are parsed from the shared parser's
argument under a strict positive-integer `NdM` grammar. Unknown tags, extra arguments, nested
formula syntax, overflow, symbolic dice and unsupported wording abstain.

## Patterns and canonical claims

| Pattern | Stable owner-scoped slot | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fireball-save-v1` | `saving-throw` | `SavingThrowRequirementMechanic`, fixed Dexterity and explicitly unknown DC (`NOT_AUTHORED`); subject unknown/not interpreted |
| `fireball-damage-v1` | `failed-save-damage` | `DamageMechanic`, `DiceAmount(8,6,0)`, authored fire type; `AppliesOnSaveOutcome(FAILURE)` bound to the exact emitted save revision |
| `healing-word-amount-v1` | `healing` | `HealingMechanic`, ordered sum of `DiceAmount(1,4,0)` and symbolic caster spellcasting ability modifier; hit-point restoration mode, unknown/not-interpreted subject |

All three claims are explicitly PARTIAL. Fireball's normalized `savingThrows` and `damageInflicted`
classifications must agree with the original source and the supported prose. Damage cannot be
emitted without its actual save claim and failure qualifier. No fixed DC, target ID, damage receiver,
or successful-save damage amount is inferred.

Healing Word's supported healing clause must include both the dice tag and the complete symbolic
modifier wording. A missing modifier never becomes dice-only healing. The sum is not evaluated.
Structured time, range, components and other existing classifications remain in their original
normalized/public fields; they are not duplicated as newly interpreted mechanics.

The bounded Fireball main paragraph and Healing Word main entry are matched completely. Changes
to qualifiers, conditional clauses or tag dialects abstain. This conservative coupling can suppress
a still-valid individual fact when another part of the supported paragraph changes; that is an
intentional pilot limitation, not a claim of general parsing capability. Synthetic in-memory tests
change dice values and demonstrate source-derived parsing; changed bytes cannot pass the runtime pin.

## Evidence and provenance

Every claim binds source revision, owner, JSON Pointer, extraction pattern/revision, typed payload
and context through the existing canonical claim hash. Main and higher-level normalized text and
supported section hierarchy are checked against original bytes. Source/resource, normalization or
canonical integrity mismatches fail startup; pattern mismatches become controlled abstentions.

| Owner | Unique evidence records | Claim associations | Unsupported concepts |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Fireball PHB | 7 | 4 | 3 |
| Healing Word PHB | 3 | 1 | 2 |
| Shield PHB | 13 | 17 | 0 |
| Shield XPHB | 13 | 17 | 0 |

Text evidence retains the complete containing-string hash and exact Java UTF-16 `[start,end)`
span, source hierarchy, source fingerprint and authored owner. Structured evidence uses its actual
field and value hash, without invented text spans. Evidence associations identify payload or context
paths. Local evidence IDs are opaque, owner/pointer/fingerprint-bound identifiers, not mechanic IDs.

Fireball's damage-role span includes formula, fire label and failure wording; the failure span also
supports the save-outcome binding. Saving-throw evidence combines its authored requirement clause
and `/savingThrow/0`. Healing Word's single bounded role span contains both amount terms and the
healing verb, so neither an isolated dice token nor prose proximity is used as the complete effect.

All four existing formula references for Fireball/Healing Word remain supplemental observations,
including `scaledamage` and `scaledice` in higher-level entries. No formula is replaced, reindexed,
evaluated, or treated as an independently sufficient effect. Claim and observation identities remain
distinct. Complete Spell prose is not copied into normal inspection responses.

include a checked absence; they do not claim an absent field has authored evidence. Other 965 owners
remain NOT_ATTEMPTED / NOT_ASSESSED, including Magic Missile and Prestidigitation.
