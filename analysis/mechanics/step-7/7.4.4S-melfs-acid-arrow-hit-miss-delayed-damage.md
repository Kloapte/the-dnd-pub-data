## Model changes

- `TargetScope.SINGLE_TARGET_UNSPECIFIED_CATEGORY` and `SpellTargetSubject` express one target while
  leaving its category unspecified. The PHB text does not establish creature-only or object-only
  eligibility.
- `DamageTiming` distinguishes `IMMEDIATE` from `END_OF_TARGET_NEXT_TURN`. The delayed timing is an
  application time, rather than a duration, so the existing `UntilTurnBoundary` duration type is not
  repurposed.
- `SpellSlotDamageScaling` holds a dice increment, base slot level, minimum casting slot level and
  interval. It describes one `1d4` increment beginning above slot level 2 and does not change attack
  count.
- `FractionOfAmount` holds `1/2` and a revision-bound reference to the initial HIT damage claim. It
  inherits that claim's spell-slot scaling, creates no second dice roll, and states no rounding rule.
- `MechanicsBindings` includes the fractional base reference in integrity and effective-review
  propagation. `MechanicsDocumentValidator` requires a current same-owner damage claim of the same
  damage type, rejects stale references and fractional reference chains, and requires delayed damage
  to bind to one single-attack HIT outcome.
- New fields are nullable and omitted from old payloads when absent. Existing `DamageMechanic`
  serialization and prior claim hashes remain unchanged.

The immediate and delayed damage claims each carry the same typed spell-slot damage progression.
The MISS claim references the immediate HIT claim, so the half fraction is over its full initial
amount at any supported slot level. The separate HIT and MISS outcome claims cannot both be the
trigger for one damage claim. The delayed claim binds only to HIT; the explicit MISS clause evidence
states that no damage occurs at the end of the target's next turn.
