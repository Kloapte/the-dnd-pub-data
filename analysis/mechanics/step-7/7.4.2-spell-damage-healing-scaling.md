# Step 7.4.2 — Spell Damage, Healing and Scaling (complete)

## Scope and result

The PHB Spell extractor records bounded higher-level damage or healing increments in immutable
`SpellSlotScalingMechanic` claims. Claims retain the authored dice increment, base slot level,
minimum applicable slot level, interval and source evidence. The extractor does not calculate a
cast result.

The catalog processes 23 of 969 Spell owners: the original 13 plus ten additional PHB Spells. It
contains 64 typed mechanics, including 17 scaling claims (11 existing and six new). The ten new
owners are Blight, Chain Lightning, Cloudkill, Fire Storm, Flaming Sphere, Hellish Rebuke, Incendiary
Cloud, Insect Plague, Moonbeam and Wind Wall.

`AppliesOnSaveOutcome` remains attached to the relevant `DamageMechanic` and references the exact
saving-throw claim. Its qualifier evidence records full damage on failure and half damage on success.
These outcomes are now available for all ten new owners, alongside the six previously supported
damage owners. Explicit no-damage-on-success remains covered by a changed-source Fireball test; no
unchanged owner in the registered set currently uses that wording.

All added claims remain PENDING_REVIEW and NEEDS_REVIEW for licensing. Benchmark fixtures and
publication authorization were not changed.

## Verification

- Focused PHB extractor tests passed.
- Private inspection API tests passed for existing and new owners, including Blight damage outcomes
  and slot scaling.
- Candidate benchmark diagnostics passed; fixtures remain unchanged and pending human review.
- Full Maven suite: passed, 752 tests, zero failures and zero errors.
- Scaling tests cover all 17 claims, including the dice increment, base slot, minimum casting level,
  interval and source span.
- Source JSON files were not modified; evidence remains fingerprint-bound to the pinned owners.

## Limitations

- No-damage-on-success is covered by a changed-source positive test, but no unchanged registered
  Spell owner currently uses that wording.
- The damage pattern requires one explicit save ability, one damage formula and one locally
  associated damage type. Candidate owners with multiple or ambiguous damage/save clauses remain
  unattempted, including Blade Barrier, Otiluke's Freezing Sphere and Thunderwave.
- Other riders such as conditions, movement, targeting and area remain outside the typed claims.
  Higher-level text that changes targets, uses multiple damage formulas, or misses the bounded
  wording pattern remains unsupported.
- Existing pending benchmark fixtures do not cover the expansion. Candidate output does not
  represent accuracy and fixtures remain pending human review.

Step 7.4.2 is complete for this bounded expansion. Do not begin Step 7.4.3.
