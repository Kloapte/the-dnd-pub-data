# Step 7.4.4I — Cantrip Damage Scaling

**Status: COMPLETE.** This step starts with
`spell:phb:ray-of-frost`; any extension is limited to already-processed, already-eligible owners
whose source semantics pass the checks below.

## Preimplementation model decision

`SpellSlotScalingMechanic` from Step 7.4.2 describes an authored per-slot-level increment. Applying
it to cantrips would conflate spell-slot upcasting with character-level progression. The existing
`DamageMechanic.amount` represents the base dice amount but cannot preserve alternate damage totals
at character-level thresholds. The Spell import model already retains structured `scalingLevelDice`
as `SpellScaling`, but it is not a canonical mechanics payload and does not represent its
relationship to one interpreted damage claim.

The selected minimal extension is an optional typed `CharacterLevelDamageScaling` on
`DamageMechanic`, containing ordered `CharacterLevelDamageTier` values. The existing damage `amount`
is the base amount beginning at character level 1; each tier gives the complete replacement dice
amount from its minimum character level through the level before the next tier. This avoids
duplicating the base formula or creating a separate scaling claim, and keeps slot-level scaling
separate. Tiers are descriptive canonical data, not a damage evaluator. Only explicit structured
and prose-agreeing progression is emitted.

## Implementation

Added `CharacterLevelDamageScaling` and `CharacterLevelDamageTier` as immutable typed values on
`DamageMechanic`. The base `amount` is the complete formula from level 1 until the first tier; each
tier is the complete replacement formula from its inclusive threshold. Validation requires ordered
thresholds and increasing dice counts on the same die and modifier. This model does not evaluate
damage and does not reuse `SpellSlotScalingMechanic`.

Only `spell:phb:ray-of-frost` was changed. Its existing
`mechanic:spell:phb:ray-of-frost:damage` claim advances from revision 1
(`126f7880b1895357a0a51913971f334e071754f18931be4266f0c1427d952d2a`) to revision 2
(`0d5b114353dddebca4959aaf8eff8a39972c76b6bb67b14ba4d697e3ed855240`). Revision 2 explicitly
supersedes the captured revision 1 reference, keeps the same mechanic ID, and changes the content
hash to bind the new typed scaling and evidence. The damage remains bound to the HIT outcome of
`mechanic:spell:phb:ray-of-frost:attack-roll` revision 1. That attack claim is unchanged at
`41850c02d6434cae5b0c9f1d05b2459ec970cea11e69e4ef25f0c0504e2451f9`.

The progression is represented by one damage claim:

| 5–10 | 2d8 cold |
| 11–16 | 3d8 cold |
| 17–20 | 4d8 cold |

The `ray-of-frost-cantrip-scaling` unsupported diagnostic is removed because the full supported
progression is represented. The existing `mechanic:spell:phb:ray-of-frost:movement-speed` claim
