# Step 7.4.3B — Spell Condition Dependencies and Endings (partial)

## Scope and result

This bounded batch enriched five already processed PHB Spell owners: Hold Person,
Blindness/Deafness, Fear, Greater Invisibility, and Hypnotic Pattern. It adds four typed
`SavingThrowRequirementMechanic` claims and keeps the catalog at 87 processed owners and 160 typed
mechanics. No pinned JSON, benchmark fixtures, or public REST defaults changed.

New context relationships include `ConditionOnSaveOutcome`, `ConditionEndsOnSaveSuccess`,
`ConditionEndsWithSpell`, `RequiresConcentration`, and the existing `AppliesForDuration`. Save links
use revision-checked references and exact source evidence. The saving throw DC remains unknown.
`SpellTargetSubject` records a single target, multiple selected creatures, area-affected creatures,
or the caster-or-touched choice; Hold Person retains its Humanoid restriction. Durations reference
the source Spell duration record, and concentration is a separate source-backed relationship.

Hold Person and Blindness/Deafness now bind failed initial saves and successful end-of-turn repeat
saves. Blindness/Deafness keeps the single exclusive choice group as the effect carrier, so save and
duration relationships apply to the selected condition. Fear binds the failed Wisdom save and area
scope, with concentration and duration. Its movement and conditional line-of-sight ending remain
exact text evidence and are explicitly unsupported as typed endings. Greater Invisibility records
the caster-or-touched target, declared duration, concentration, and ending with the spell. Hypnotic
Pattern binds the failed save to Charmed only; Incapacitated remains simultaneous and is not given a
separate failed-save dependency. Its damage/action endings remain explicitly unsupported.

Claims retain stable IDs; changed payload/context content is reflected in recomputed claim and
document hashes. New and revised claims remain PENDING_REVIEW and NEEDS_REVIEW for licensing. No
claim is verified or publication-authorized.

expiration. Unsupported target grammar and more complex save timing remain outside this batch.
