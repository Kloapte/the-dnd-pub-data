# Step 7.4.4 Closure Remediation

**Scope:** approved fixes F-02, F-03, F-04 and F-06 from the [consolidation readiness review](7.4.4-consolidation-readiness-review.md).
F-01 was accepted without work. F-05 remains deferred. This report does not close Step 7.4.4.

## 1. Implementation decisions

### F-02 — Order-independent document hashes

Document hash protocol 2 sorts the `interpretations` list by the stable, owner-scoped mechanic ID
before canonical serialization. The hash input has a version-specific domain prefix. Evidence,
observations, source revisions and other lists retain their existing order because their order can
carry source, derivation or projection meaning. Duplicate mechanic IDs continue to be rejected by
document validation before canonical document creation. Individual claim hashing remains unchanged.

The migration is explicit in `MechanicsDocumentRevision.hashProtocolVersion`. Newly created
documents use protocol 2. A serialized legacy revision without this field is read as protocol 1,
whose exact historical serialization remains available. Explicit versions 1 and 2 are accepted;
unknown versions are rejected. There is no silent reinterpretation of a version-1 hash as version 2.

The v2 migration intentionally changes canonical document serialization and hash values for all
969 Spell owners. New documents identify their protocol as version 2. The private inspection and
default REST projections do not expose document revisions. Private inspection adds a declared scope
extent for all 129 processed owners, and the three cantrip owners also receive the assessment
corrections described under F-04. The three PHB cantrip attack claims now have
PARTIAL completeness to expose their unrepresented attack range; their dependent claim references
are regenerated with the corresponding current hashes. That bounded semantic correction changes
claim content hashes for the 11 claims belonging to those three owners. No other claim payloads,
identities or revisions were migrated.

### F-03 — Generic conditional attack-chain validation

`MechanicsDocumentValidator` now checks shared invariants: exact current attack and damage references,
one initial attack opportunity, shared target context, HIT-bound reusable damage, a matching-dice
trigger bound to that damage, enough matching-size dice in the amount expression, and a nondecreasing
spell-slot leap limit. `ConditionalAttackChainMechanic` retains declarative structural checks and no
longer hardcodes the Chromatic Orb convention that the initial attack is excluded from the leap
limit.

Chromatic Orb's source-specific interpretation remains in `XphbChromaticOrbRules`: cast-time ranged
spell attack, d8 matching trigger, its target reuse and previous-target distance constraints, slot
progression, damage type choice timing and persistence. The generic validator does not execute attack
chains, resolve targets, interpret arbitrary scripts or add saving-throw chaining.

A model test now successfully validates a different configuration: a bonus-action melee spell
attack, 2d6 fire damage, a d6 match trigger, a different slot limit and a limit that counts the
initial attack. Existing Chromatic Orb tests continue to assert its original source-specific values.

### F-04 — Source accounting and review readiness

The coverage model adds optional `scopeExtent` (`FULL_SPELL` or `DECLARED_SUBSET`). Legacy JSON
omits the field and remains readable. `COMPLETE_WITHIN_SCOPE` now requires an explicit extent,
nonempty considered paths and no unassessed paths. Unsupported diagnostics and PARTIAL individual
claims are allowed in a complete source-accounting scope: complete means all declared source content
has been accounted for, not that every mechanic is represented or correct. Existing scope status is
not promoted merely because paths were inspected.

New partial scopes from supported extraction adapters are explicitly marked `DECLARED_SUBSET`,
while complete PHB cantrip scopes are `FULL_SPELL`. The legacy constructor continues to omit this
field for older payloads. The private inspection projection has an optional
`readyForHumanReviewWithinScope` value. It is
present and true only for a successfully processed `COMPLETE_WITHIN_SCOPE`; it does not alter claim
review, licensing or publication state. The three cantrip scopes are explicitly `FULL_SPELL` and
list source paths for identity, level/school, casting data, components, duration/range, structured
mechanics fields, tags, entries and higher-level entries. Their melee/ranged attack distance is not
represented by the attack mechanic, so each has a source-evidenced `FAMILY_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`
ATTACK_ROLL diagnostic, and its attack claim remains PARTIAL.

| PHB owner | Before | After | Accounting and review consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `spell:phb:chill-touch` | PARTIAL; 4 complete claims; no unsupported diagnostic | COMPLETE_WITHIN_SCOPE / FULL_SPELL; attack claim PARTIAL; 1 attack-range diagnostic | Scope is accounted and ready for human review; unresolved attack range remains explicit. |
| `spell:phb:ray-of-frost` | PARTIAL; 3 complete claims; no unsupported diagnostic | COMPLETE_WITHIN_SCOPE / FULL_SPELL; attack claim PARTIAL; 1 attack-range diagnostic | Same distinction; its damage and speed-reduction claims remain assessed separately. |
| `spell:phb:shocking-grasp` | PARTIAL; 4 complete claims; no unsupported diagnostic | COMPLETE_WITHIN_SCOPE / FULL_SPELL; attack claim PARTIAL; 1 attack-range diagnostic | Same distinction; metal-armor advantage and reaction prevention remain assessed separately. |

All three owners still have `PENDING_REVIEW` recorded claims. Licensing remains `NEEDS_REVIEW` and
publication authorization remains zero. The extraction completeness, interpretation completeness,
human review and licensing decisions are separate.

### F-06 — Source-backed evidence validation

Ordinary model/document construction remains structural and does not mark evidence source-verified.
`MechanicsSourceEvidenceVerifier` is the explicit source-aware boundary: callers provide raw source
bytes and trusted pinned revisions. It confirms byte fingerprints, exact document revisions,
source owner collection/root pointers against the pinned owner manifest, authored source identity
and page, evidence pointers, text and structured-value hashes, span bounds and UTF-16 surrogate
boundaries. `MechanicsSourceContent` defensively copies bytes. The extraction service validates each
manifest owner whose revision is referenced before calling the verifier; arbitrary deserialized
evidence receives no verification flag and is not trusted merely because it passed DTO construction.

Added negative cases cover out-of-range spans, absent structured pointers, byte/revision mismatch,
untrusted manifest revisions, and a span splitting a surrogate pair. A valid raw pinned source passes.
The check intentionally validates authored text and structured-field evidence; transformed evidence
still relies on the existing evidence-input graph and transformation records.
