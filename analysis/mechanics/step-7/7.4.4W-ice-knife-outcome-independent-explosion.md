## Attack, piercing damage, and explosion

The attack claim represents one ranged spell attack by the caster during casting against one
creature. It has no repeated activation, multi-instance count progression, or automatic HIT. The
spell’s casting range is not substituted for an attack-range field, because the attack model has no
typed range qualifier. `ice-knife-unsupported:attack-range` records this missing range constraint
with evidence from the target wording and structured spell range. The attack claim remains PARTIAL.

The separate piercing-damage claim is `1d10` piercing, bound only to HIT on the exact attack claim.
It has no spell-slot scaling and no MISS damage binding.

One `AttackTriggeredAreaEffectMechanic` claim references the same initial attack and uses the typed
trigger `AFTER_ATTACK_REGARDLESS_OF_OUTCOME`. It occurs once per attack. Since Ice Knife makes one
initial attack per casting, the area effect follows once per casting. This relationship does not
depend on HIT damage and cannot branch into duplicate HIT and MISS explosions.

The area is centered on the attack target, has a 5-foot radius, includes the target, and affects
creatures. It is a single instantaneous effect, not an attack roll, persistent effect, or per-creature
effect claim. The affected-creature subject is shared by the save and cold-damage claims. No caster
exclusion or friendly-fire exception is represented.

## Saving throw, cold damage, and scaling

The explosion’s saving-throw claim requires Dexterity saves from creatures affected by the area,
including the original target. It references the explosion rather than an attack outcome. The spell
text does not specify a save DC or its attribution, so the typed difficulty remains
`NOT_AUTHORED`; `ice-knife-unsupported:save-dc-attribution` records that limitation. The save claim is
PARTIAL.

The separate cold-damage claim is `2d6` cold on a failed save and explicitly `NO_DAMAGE` on a
successful save. It references both the explosion and its saving-throw claim. It is not bound to
initial HIT/MISS and does not add an attack roll.

`SpellSlotDamageScaling(1d6, base 1, minimum 2, interval 1)` applies only to the cold damage. Slots
1–9 yield `2d6` through `10d6` on a failed save. Initial piercing damage remains `1d10`; attack
count, explosion radius, and successful-save damage do not scale.
