## Model and extraction

The canonical model adds these immutable types in `mechanics.model`:

- `AttackRollMechanic` carries `AttackKind`, actor role, a fixed count, whether those attacks are independent, and activation timing. The pilot records `MELEE_SPELL`, `CASTER`, one independent attack, and `CAST_TIME`.
- `AttackKind` distinguishes melee/ranged spell and weapon attacks. `AttackActorRole` gives the actor an explicit typed role. The pilot does not use non-caster roles or weapon attacks.
- `AttackRollOutcome` defines `HIT` and `MISS`; `CRITICAL_HIT` is a general outcome value, but no critical-hit rule or dependency is inferred for this Spell.
- `AppliesOnAttackRollOutcome` binds an effect to an exact `MechanicRevisionReference`, outcome and evidence IDs. The document validator requires the binding on a damage claim, validates its target type, and rejects a stale revision reference.

The attack context uses the existing `SpellTargetSubject(SINGLE_TARGET_CREATURE)`. No combat engine, generic properties, target-selection engine, or attack-resolution rules were added. Existing `SpellMechanicsEvidenceSource`, `SpellMechanicsAssembly`, revision hashing, review metadata and inspection projection are reused.

## Deferred gaps and exclusions

No other attack-roll owner is activated. Multiple or repeated attacks, level-scaled attack counts, object targets, weapon attacks, summoned actors, miss damage, special critical thresholds, advantage/disadvantage, roll replacement and additional on-hit effects remain outside the pilot. The typed model records general outcomes but does not assign unobserved results or infer critical-hit behavior. Higher-level damage scaling remains unmodeled, and broader candidate eligibility remains an offline assessment concern. Human review, licensing assessment and any future publication decision remain separate.

The current outcome binding is deliberately validated for damage claims only. Supporting hit-dependent condition or modifier effects will require a reviewed extension of that relationship validation and corresponding evidence-role tests. The pilot's `SINGLE_TARGET_CREATURE` scope also does not encode reach or a more specific targeting constraint; those remain source facts in the evidence rather than executable targeting rules.
