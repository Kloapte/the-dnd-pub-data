## Scope and result

`GRANT_IMMUNITY` operations, and typed values for Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Grappled,
Incapacitated, Invisible, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Stunned, and Unconscious.
The mechanic is part of strict polymorphic serialization, quality checks, benchmark family mapping,
and the existing private Spell inspection projection.

The reusable PHB pattern maps the source's explicit `conditionInflict` and `conditionImmune`
classifications separately. Each claim is partial, uses a stable source-aware mechanic ID, and cites
the exact source field. When the condition appears in a top-level text entry, its full sentence is
also retained as a qualifier with an exact text span. New claims remain PENDING_REVIEW and
NEEDS_REVIEW for licensing.

The catalog now processes 87 of 969 Spell owners and has 155 typed mechanics, including the previous
64 mechanics and 91 condition claims. This adds 64 PHB owners with condition classification metadata.
Condition claims do not assert immediate application: target and trigger context remain unknown, and
the sentence qualifier is retained as authored text rather than normalized into an execution rule.

## Limitations and abstentions

- The `REMOVE` operation is modeled distinctly but no removal extraction pattern is enabled in this
  batch. Removal chosen from alternatives (for example, “end one condition from this list”) remains
  unsupported until choice and target context can be represented safely.
- Structured condition classifications do not provide typed target restrictions, save dependencies,
  recurring saves, concentration, or multi-sentence ending conditions. Same-sentence text from
  supported top-level entries is kept verbatim as a partial qualifier; when such text is unavailable,
  the source classification claim remains partial without that qualifier. Unsupported condition
  names abstain.
- Immunity classification and condition application are distinct claims; this is not a gameplay
  execution engine and does not claim that all modifiers or effects are covered.
- Benchmark fixtures are unchanged and still await human review. Existing candidate diagnostics do
  not measure condition extraction accuracy.
