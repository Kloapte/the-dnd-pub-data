# Step 7.5 — Generic Extraction Strategy Gap Analysis

## 1. Executive summary

The v2 pipeline is a sound experimental scaffold for source-fragment collection, six-family candidate classification, shallow typed payload extraction, deterministic projection and evidence deduplication. It has **not migrated the existing canonical extraction capabilities** into an owner-independent interpreter. Only leaf forms of attack, damage, saving throw, condition application and healing are partially generic. Scaling is recognized but never extracted. Outcome, target, duration, activation, attack-chain, area and other cross-mechanic semantics remain absent from v2 composition.

The canonical implementation still has eight registered top-level adapters. Six are dedicated to a single source-qualified owner each; the PHB extractor and Shield extractor cover owner sets but retain explicit owner/source gates. The experimental v2 interpreter is independent of those adapters and is source/owner agnostic, but its current strategy grammar is too shallow to reproduce most canonical payloads or their semantic context.

The stated comparison is accurate only at the **payload** level. Comparing the 279 canonical claim payloads one-to-one against same-owner v2 candidate payloads yields 22 exact payload matches, 257 without an exact match (74 with no remaining same-family candidate, 150 with different same-family payloads, 33 outside a v2 family). The 22 are all condition payloads. Exact payload equality does not establish equivalent target context, dependencies, evidence or meaning. The 1,004 additional candidate mechanics are candidate payloads, not validated interpretations.

No production behavior was changed. The recommended path is to migrate reusable semantics in dependency order, starting with source-backed relationships between saves, attacks, damage and outcomes, and only then add broad activation, scaling and effect families.

## 3. Complete legacy extraction capability inventory

The registered production pipeline is `SpellMechanicsPatternRegistry.standard()` in `src/main/java/com/thedndpub/data/mechanics/spell/SpellMechanicsPatternRegistry.java`. It registers, in order, `ShieldMechanicsExtractor`, `PhbSpiritualWeaponRules`, `PhbWitchBoltRules`, `PhbSpellMechanicsExtractor`, `XgeSteelWindStrikeRules`, `XgeMagicStoneRules`, `XgeIceKnifeRules`, and `XphbChromaticOrbRules`. The registry enforces at most one matching top-level production pattern per owner and rejects overlap. These are adapters, not eight generic rules.

`PhbSpellMechanicsExtractor` is the broad adapter, but it is explicitly dispatched through owner sets (`DAMAGE_OWNERS`, `HEALING_OWNERS`, `CONDITION_DEPENDENCY_OWNERS`, `MODIFIER_AND_PROTECTION_OWNERS`, `GENERALIZED_SAVE_DAMAGE_OWNERS`, `GENERALIZED_CONDITION_REMOVAL_OWNERS`, and structured-immunity assessment owners). Its `extractSupported` routes exact Magic Missile, attack-owner, immunity-owner, save/damage, condition, and named modifier branches. `supportsForInventory` is intentionally broader than production eligibility. Inventory applicability must not be confused with production extraction.

Important helper implementations:

- `PhbSaveDamageGeneralizer.extract` uses `savingThrow`, `locateSave`, `locateTarget`, `locateComponents`, `saveOutcomes` and slot-scaling routines. It ties a typed save to damage outcomes for supported PHB prose and parses individual typed damage components. It rejects multiple/ambiguous relationships and remains behind PHB owner gates.
- `PhbSpellAttackRules.extract`, `attackRoll`, and `hitDamage` handle a small simple-attack rule table; `extractScorchingRay` and `scorchingRayDamage` model independent rays; `extractEldritchBlast` models character-level attack count; `extractMelfsAcidArrow` models HIT/MISS and delayed branches. The rule table and special branches are owner-specific.
- `PhbCantripDamageScaling` performs source-backed character-level scaling for bounded cantrip claims.
- `PhbConditionMechanicsRules` handles condition application/removal, exclusive choices, save-linked condition effects, repeat-save/ending qualifiers and structured condition immunity. Its package-private extraction entry points are called by the owner-gated PHB dispatcher. Its bounded phrase recognizers include explicit `conditionRemovalChoice`, `explicitConditionRemoval`, `genericConditionTarget` and condition-save/end parsing.
- `PhbModifierAndProtectionRules.extractModifierAndProtection` dispatches named extraction paths for AC bonuses/floors/replacements, attack/save roll bonuses or penalties, speed increase, selected resistance and nonmagical weapon resistance. Methods include `shieldOfFaith`, `mageArmor`, `barkskin`, `longstrider`, `bless`, `bane`, `baneSavingThrow`, `protectionFromEnergy`, and `stoneskin`. This is not a general modifier grammar.
- `PhbMagicMissileRules` models automatic-hit projectile series, per-dart damage, target scope, and slot-based dart count for the exact PHB owner.
- `ShieldMechanicsExtractor` handles PHB and XPHB Shield separately: reaction activation, AC modifier, effect duration/turn boundary, and Magic Missile prevention. Source/edition and owner checks are explicit.
- Six single-owner patterns add bounded specialized models: `PhbSpiritualWeaponRules` (persistent effect and repeated attacks); `PhbWitchBoltRules` (HIT-established link, optional action activation, initial/subsequent damage and termination); `XgeSteelWindStrikeRules` (selected-target independent attacks and damage); `XgeMagicStoneRules` (finite enchanted objects, alternate attack actor and per-use consumption); `XgeIceKnifeRules` (outcome-independent explosion, area/save/damage); and `XphbChromaticOrbRules` (damage choice, matching-die trigger and bounded conditional chain).

Shared legacy infrastructure (`SpellMechanicsAssembly`, `SpellMechanicsEvidenceSource`, `SpellMechanicsExtractionService`, claim validators and source evidence verification) provides claim identity, evidence associations, dependency references, hash validation, completeness, unsupported diagnostics and review/licensing state. Those are supporting contracts, not mechanic extraction strategies.

## 4. Legacy-to-generic mapping

The machine-readable capability matrix contains the complete family inventory and names current legacy, recognizer, strategy and composition layers. The essential mapping is:

| Capability | v2 implementation | Classification | Key gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Save requirement | structured/text signal → `GenericMechanicStrategy.save` | `PARTIALLY_GENERIC` | Single ability only; difficulty, target and dependency are absent. |
| Save success/failure semantics | None | `EXTRACTED_RELATIONSHIP_MISSING` | No bindings for half/no/full damage or condition onset/ending. |
| Damage | signal → dice-only `GenericMechanicStrategy.damage` | `PARTIALLY_GENERIC` | Damage type, target and application event are unresolved. |
| Attack roll | signal → one cast-time attack strategy | `PARTIALLY_GENERIC` | Limited one cast-time attack grammar; no independent groups or activations. |
| Healing | lexical signal → hit-point restoration strategy | `PARTIALLY_GENERIC` | Single dice amount; no additive expression, target or scaling. |
| Condition application | structured `conditionInflict` → APPLY | `PARTIALLY_GENERIC` | No authored operation/context proof; no remove, immunity, choice or duration. |
| Condition removal/immunity/choice | No v2 strategy | `LEGACY_ONLY` | PHB bounded grammar and owner gates. |
| Slot scaling | classified `SCALING`; strategy returns unsupported | `RECOGNIZED_NOT_EXTRACTED` | No scaling payload or affected-mechanic association. |
| Cantrip scaling | No v2 extraction | `LEGACY_ONLY` | PHB-specific character-level progression. |
| Modifiers/protection/resistance/reaction | No v2 type/strategy | `LEGACY_ONLY` | Model payloads do not imply extraction. |
| Areas, persistent effects, repeated/independent attacks, objects, triggered damage, conditional chains | No applicable v2 strategy | `LEGACY_ONLY` | Requires relationship/event semantics beyond six v2 families. |
| Source evidence/candidate projection | fragment normalization and evidence aggregation | `PARTIALLY_GENERIC` | Whole-fragment spans and candidate-level support; not equivalent to phrase-level legacy source verification. |

There are six `MechanicType` values and one `GenericMechanicStrategy` implementation parameterized by each value. The `MechanicStrategyRegistry` allows multiple strategies per type, but `standard()` currently registers only one per type. Thus six configured strategy entries are not six fully generic capabilities. In particular, the SCALING strategy has no successful extraction path.

### v2 normalization, classification, extraction and composition

- `MechanicSourceNormalizer.normalize` reads structured fields `spellAttack`, `savingThrow`, `damageInflict`, `conditionInflict`, `conditionImmune`, `damageResist`, `damageVulnerable`, `abilityCheck`, `areaTags`, `miscTags`; context fields are retained; nested `entries` and `entriesHigherLevel` are walked. Authored hierarchy is retained for text fragments.
- Structured damage resistance/vulnerability are currently classified as `DAMAGE`, not as a resistance operation. `areaTags`/`miscTags` produce no candidate. `abilityCheck` is labeled `SAVING_THROW`, an acknowledged type conflation.
- `MechanicTypeDeterminer` simply emits one candidate per preassigned fragment signal (`generic-source-signals:1`). It is a candidate signal expander, not semantic recognition.
- `GenericMechanicStrategy` extracts shallow leaf payloads. Attack requires cast-time language and one attack phrase; damage accepts one `{@damage NdS}` amount and does not establish type or outcome; save accepts one ability and uses unknown DC; healing accepts one dice formula; condition converts structured `conditionInflict` to APPLY; scaling always returns unsupported.
- `GenericMechanicsRecognitionPipeline.compose` runs all candidates, merges identical payloads, reports conflicting payloads, deduplicates evidence by resource/pointer, and reports co-occurrence for mechanics sharing a fragment. It does not connect damage to attack/save, scaling to an amount, or condition to a save/duration. Co-occurrence is explicitly not a dependency.

## 5. Missing recognizers and strategies

1. **Attack semantics:** attacks represented by authored forms beyond “one attack as part of casting”; attack actor, target count, independent rolls, HIT outcomes, attack timing and attack-specific modifiers need source-backed strategies.
2. **Damage semantics:** typed damage type choices, symbolic modifiers/sums, separate components, HIT/MISS/save triggers, delayed damage, and one damage event per attack/target are absent from generic strategies.
3. **Outcome and effect relations:** save result to damage/condition; attack HIT/MISS to damage/effect; damage event to subsequent trigger; onset and ending conditions are missing.
4. **Scaling:** slot-level and character-level progression must parse exact increments/thresholds and bind to the affected amount or count. It must distinguish damage, target count, attack count and other scaling axes.
5. **Condition operations:** apply, remove, immunity, choice, repeat saves, ending, target restrictions and duration are distinct operations, not interchangeable condition keywords.
6. **Healing:** additive amounts and symbolic ability modifiers, target/eligibility, cap/temporary HP distinctions, and scaling are needed.
7. **Modifiers and prevention:** AC/statistic operations, resistance/vulnerability, damage prevention, condition immunity, reaction costs/triggers, and exemptions need typed recognizers.
8. **Effect shape:** target selection, areas/centers, range/reach, multiple independent targets, persistent effects, concentration, duration, repeatable activations, summoned/enchanted objects, resource use and conditional attack chaining have no generic v2 extraction.

## 6. Missing composition capabilities

Composition is the central migration gap, not just another collection of parsers. `GenericMechanicsRecognitionPipeline` currently lets overlapping candidates be associated only as `CO_OCCURS_IN_SOURCE_FRAGMENT`. Required reusable composition abilities include:

- Evidence-backed selection of which save governs which damage/condition component, including multiple components or saves.
- Exact HIT/MISS and save outcome bindings with claim revision/hash references.
- Damage-to-attack event binding that permits reusable damage definitions without a cyclic claim graph.
- Scaling-to-specific-payload binding while keeping independent scale axes separate.
- Duration/concentration binding to a particular effect, and explicit effect establishment/termination.
- Attack-group composition for independent attacks, selected targets and bounded conditional chains.
- Event ordering for delayed, outcome-independent and manually activated effects, without turning static claim dependencies into runtime execution graphs.
- Conflict detection at the relationship level. Current same-fragment conflict checks compare different strategy payloads only for one fragment/type; they do not identify contradictory source interpretations across fragments.

## 7. Canonical versus v2 discrepancy analysis

### Deterministic per-claim results

The companion JSON contains all 279 canonical claims from 129 processed owners, each with owner, claim ID/revision, kind, canonical payload, v2 family/candidate IDs, difference paths and a reason category. Its four categories reconcile to 279:

| Comparison result | Claims | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Exact payload | 22 | All are `CONDITION_CHANGE`; payload-only equality. |
| No same-family candidate | 74 | v2 family exists, but no unconsumed same-owner candidate of the family was emitted. Includes 23 slot-scaling claims because v2 scaling never extracts and one damage-series claim because a scalar damage family cannot represent the series. |
| Same-family payload differs | 150 | v2 candidate is present, but payload fields differ. |
| No generic v2 family | 33 | Other payload families outside v2's six-type set. |

The `DAMAGE_INSTANCE_SERIES` claim is classified under the nearest conceptual `DAMAGE` family for this comparison, but that is a mismatch: v2 has no series/count/automatic-hit model. This is why it falls under no candidate, not no generic family.

Among different payloads, the recurring differences are:

- `SAVING_THROW_REQUIREMENT`: 50 difficulty values differ (canonical frequently says `NOT_AUTHORED`, generic says `NOT_INTERPRETED`); v2 also loses canonical target context and save dependencies.
- `DAMAGE`: 32 damage types are missing/unknown, 3 amount expressions differ, 4 character-level scaling values differ, and 2 slot-scaling expressions differ. These field counts overlap within payloads.
- `CONDITION_CHANGE`: 62 qualifier lists differ, 21 condition values differ, 2 choice-group references differ, and 1 operation differs. The 22 exact condition payloads still need context and evidence comparison.
- `HEALING`: all five amounts differ, because the generic strategy cannot construct the legacy typed sum with caster spellcasting modifier.
- `ATTACK_ROLL`: one activation pattern and one actor role differ across same-family candidate comparisons.

### Cause attribution

The per-claim comparison supports these direct conclusions: 33 claims belong to families with no v2 extraction family; 23 scaling claims are recognized but not extracted; 150 same-family payloads differ; and 74 claims have no corresponding unconsumed candidate. These are not mutually exclusive root causes for the whole 257: source predicates, owner-gated legacy branches, missing context, granularity and representation all intersect.

The 150 same-family differences are chiefly **different typed representation/semantic granularity**, not necessarily v2 errors: the generic pipeline deliberately leaves type, outcome, DC and context unresolved where it cannot establish them. Some are missing extraction capability (e.g. symbolic healing sums); some are incomplete v2 recognition (e.g. no matching single cast-time attack form); some could be source-specific legacy assumptions. Assigning a more exact root cause to each claim requires replaying both extractors against that claim's exact pinned source path and comparing evidence/semantics; the machine matrix therefore preserves claim-specific mismatch paths without overclaiming root cause.

The `canonicalComparison` metadata is also an implementation limitation: it computes owner-local payload counts and same-class differences, but it does not compare claim IDs, dependencies, context or evidence. The summary artifact's “257 not reproduced” should therefore be read as payload-only.

## 8. Proposed reusable strategy boundaries

Keep the pipeline's five responsibilities distinct and migrate semantics in separable units:

1. **Normalization:** retain source-qualified owner, exact source revision, pointer, entry hierarchy, markup token spans, structured field identity and parent/neighbor context. Common markup parsing should return typed source observations, not mechanic payloads.
2. **Type determination:** emit multiple typed candidates with evidence-bearing signals and explicit confidence/ambiguity reasons. Structured tags are candidate inputs; authored phrases and their contextual clause establish operation and scope. Distinguish saves from ability checks and damage from resistance/prevention.
3. **Extraction strategies:** strategy families should own one semantic responsibility (attack form; amount/damage component; save requirement; condition operation; healing expression; scaling rule; modifier/prevention; duration/activation; area/target). Their inputs should be source fragments plus a bounded interpretation context. They must abstain when required operands/relations are absent.
4. **Relationship composition:** a separate composer should connect typed extracted nodes only through explicit, evidence-backed links. Preserve exact revision/hash refs and keep static claim DAGs acyclic. A relationship rule may describe a runtime sequence without making a dependency cycle.
5. **Validation/projection:** validate source ownership, evidence span, payload invariants and cross-claim edges before projecting. Dedupe source evidence at evidence identity level; retain all support roles and mechanics links.

Existing reusable legacy code worth extracting first: amount/dice parsing and balanced markup location; supported save ability and result phrase parsing; damage-component/type association; explicit condition-tag/operation parsing; slot-scaling interval extraction. The extraction should first remove owner dispatch from the grammar while keeping source/edition checks where the source grammar requires them. The following require redesign rather than mechanical copying: named modifier methods, Magic Missile series, Scorching Ray/EB count logic, attack-specific cantrip riders, Melf delayed/MISS branches, and the six dedicated owner adapters. Do not move their spell-name predicates into “generic” helpers.

Common low-level utilities can be reused across strategies: balanced markup parser and span mapper; UTF-16 source-span verifier; sentence/clause locator that retains original text offsets; dice/amount-expression parser; source pointer/evidence factory; and a typed relation-reference validator. They should not decide mechanical meaning.

## 9. Prioritized migration backlog

1. **Save + damage + outcome relation core.** Migrate the bounded PHB generalizer into generic save and damage strategies; extract exact save target, type, amount and result clauses; compose explicit failure/success bindings. Benefits Fireball and recurring save-damage forms, preserves 8 + 8 generalized claims and 30 condition-save cases. Edition grammar can remain source-profiled without owner allowlists.
2. **Attack + HIT damage relation.** Generalize single attack and HIT-bound damage, including attack actor, cast-time qualifier and one attack. Add target scope, typed damage and strict reference validation. This is prerequisite for cantrip riders and multi-attack composition.
3. **Amount expressions + slot scaling.** Reuse dice parser and symbolic sums; represent progression intervals and bind each scale to damage, attack count or other explicitly named operand. Keep cantrip character-level scaling a distinct scale axis.
4. **Condition operation/dependency family.** Split APPLY, REMOVE, IMMUNITY, choice and save-ended condition operations. Compose targets, duration, concentration, repeat save and ending predicates without treating every structured tag as application.
5. **Healing expressions.** Parse dice, fixed and symbolic sums, target category and eligibility; compose healing scaling and temporary-hit-point rules only where source supports them.
6. **Reusable modifiers and protections.** Establish an operation grammar for bonus, penalty, floor, replacement, resistance, prevention, advantage/disadvantage and reaction triggers. Migrate from named legacy methods only once source predicates are explicit and broad.
7. **Effect/activation and areas.** Add duration/concentration, triggered/repeated damage, reaction activation, target-relative area, independent attacks, finite enchanted objects and persistent effect semantics as families. These are more relational and should follow relation core validation.
8. **Conditional attack chain.** Migrate Chromatic Orb after damage-roll dice results, attack outcomes, target exclusions, distance and leap limits have composable reusable typed nodes. Keep it last among the pilots due to recursive event-sequence semantics and ambiguity around external dice.

This order protects current claim semantics and unlocks recurring source patterns before high-complexity exceptional structures. Prioritize by semantic dependency and reuse, not by candidate count.

## 10. Acceptance criteria for migration

- Every canonical adapter and helper capability is mapped to one reusable strategy or explicitly retained as a documented source-specific exception; no unaccounted extraction helper remains.
- Strategy matching depends on source structure and semantic evidence, not exact owner identity. Edition/source profiles are allowed where authored grammar differs; they must be explicit and must not merge PHB/XPHB identities.
- Negative tests prove abstention on ambiguous/multiple clauses, unsupported amounts, conflicting types, missing evidence, wrong outcome, and stale/wrong-owner references.
- Composition tests verify correct attack/save/damage/scaling/condition links, acyclicity, deterministic ordering and explicit unbound states.
- Per-claim canonical comparisons classify payload, context, evidence, dependency, identity and granularity separately. Every prior canonical claim is either semantically reproduced or has a source-backed, reviewed migration gap; payload-only equality is insufficient.
- Experimental artifacts are reproducible across all pinned resources, and no canonical document, review decision, licensing state or API default changes during migration.
- Coverage status accounts for source content, not merely the fragments a recognizer noticed. Human review, licensing and publication authorization remain separate.

## 11. Limitations and uncertainty

- This report inspects implementation and tests; it is not a fresh rules adjudication of all 129 canonical owners.
- Canonical extraction is owner-gated by design today; generic support in a helper does not imply production eligibility.
- v2 source evidence often points to the whole fragment, and structured fields are not enough to establish relationships. Evidence structurally resolving is not the same as evidence semantically supporting each qualifier.
- Some source mechanics remain represented as explicit unsupported diagnostics in canonical claims; their presence is not equivalent to extraction support.
- The comparison JSON stores payloads and mismatch paths, not every canonical context/evidence object. It is an inventory to guide deeper source-level migration, not a regression verdict.
- A few legacy branches are named by behavior but exact emitted mechanics depend on a finite owner list. Their intended reuse boundary must be established before extraction.
