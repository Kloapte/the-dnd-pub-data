## Implementation

`PhbSpellAttackRules` is a package-private PHB rule collaborator for the bounded one-roll attack and
hit-damage path. `PhbSpellMechanicsExtractor` retains orchestration and routes only its existing
Inflict Wounds owner and the individually registered Ray of Frost owner to that collaborator. The
shared evidence source and assembly still construct evidence, claims, diagnostics, revisions, and
review/licensing defaults. `MechanicsDocumentValidator` accepts an attack-outcome binding on either
a damage claim or a statistic-modifier claim, while still requiring a matching attack claim revision.
