## Persistent effect and activation model

`PersistentSpellEffectMechanic` represents the spell-created effect with a declared-duration
reference to this spell's duration entry. It records no concentration requirement, that the effect
is not an independently acting creature, that its attacks require explicit activation, and that the
effect ends when this spell is cast again. The duration bounds the effect; it does not calculate an
attack count.

One `AttackRollMechanic` uses actor role `SPELL_CREATED_EFFECT`, kind `MELEE_SPELL`, and one attack
per activation. Its `AttackActivationPattern` distinguishes the initial `CAST_TIME` opportunity
from later `BONUS_ACTION` opportunities, repeats only while the referenced effect persists, and
does not express a fixed total number of attacks. No instance-count or target-count progression is
used. The initial attack remains part of casting even though casting the spell itself takes a bonus
action.

The repeated source sentence combines moving the weapon up to 20 feet and repeating its attack in
the same bonus-action use. The existing model cannot express that movement-and-attack coupling or
the weapon's position and target reach without claiming an unsupported spatial rule. Those details
are therefore explicit unsupported constructs; the attack claim is `PARTIAL` rather than complete
within claim.
