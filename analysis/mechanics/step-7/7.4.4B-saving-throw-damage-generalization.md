# Step 7.4.4B — Generalize Saving Throw and Damage Extraction

## Scope and result

This batch used the pinned 5eTools v2.37.0 Spell corpus and the Step 7.4.4A inventory to generalize PHB save and damage extraction. The offline assessment uses the same canonical pattern and evidence services as production. It does not publish interpretations or activate the broad corpus matcher in production.

The expanded inventory contains 969 source-aware Spell identities. It finds 65 newly convertible owners and 101 additional potential typed claims over the preserved 7.4.4A baseline. A bounded set of eight PHB owners was enabled in production, adding 8 processed owners and 19 claims. The current production catalog therefore has 103 processed Spell owners and 190 claims. No previously processed owner lost candidate claims.

The original 7.4.4A reports are retained as `7.4.4A-spell-capability-inventory-baseline.json` and `.md`. The standard 7.4.4A inventory files now contain the expanded reassessment. The separate [before/after comparison](7.4.4B-saving-throw-damage-comparison.md) and machine-readable [comparison data](7.4.4B-saving-throw-damage-comparison.json) record the transitions.

## Investigation: recurring gaps

The 7.4.4A inventory identified saving throws and damage as the largest structured gaps: 344 Spell owners had a save classification without a matching existing pattern, and 324 had a damage classification without a matching pattern. Review of unmatched PHB examples grouped the gaps into reusable text forms:

| Pattern group | Source examples | Generalized handling |
|---|---|---|
| Explicit save clause with a local target | Calm Emotions; Acid Splash | Recognizes `must make` and `must succeed on`, typed save ability and a locally stated target scope. Save DC remains unknown unless authored as a supported fact. |
| Save outcome stated in the same or adjacent sentence as damage | Arms of Hadar; Call Lightning; Dissonant Whispers; Thunderwave | Binds full damage on an explicit failed-save clause and half or zero damage only where stated. |
| Multiple tagged damage components with separate types | Ice Storm | Emits one typed damage claim per source component and binds each to a shared save outcome. |
| Dice and dice-plus-integer damage amounts | Acid Splash; Disintegrate | Parses supported dice forms while preserving the source formula and damage type. Other arithmetic is left unsupported. |
| Existing single-type slot scaling | Arms of Hadar; Dissonant Whispers; Thunderwave | Reuses existing scaling infrastructure when one damage type makes the scaled component unambiguous. |

Observed but unimplemented cases include recurring damage triggers (Blade Barrier), actions that repeat or depend on later choices (Call Lightning), conditional damage and side effects (Dissonant Whispers), shared scaling over multiple damage types (Ice Storm and Flame Strike), and save-only prose with nonlocal outcomes or unsupported subject language. Their source prose remains outside the claim scope or is reported as unassessed. The inventory does not infer that such a Spell has no mechanics.

## Production boundary and reassessment

The eight production owners are Acid Splash, Arms of Hadar, Call Lightning, Calm Emotions, Disintegrate, Dissonant Whispers, Ice Storm and Thunderwave. Their save and damage claims retain source revision fingerprints, evidence associations, deterministic IDs, pending review state and `NEEDS_REVIEW` licensing defaults. The other owners discovered by the offline matcher are candidates only.

The refreshed inventory reports:

- 969 Spell identities; PHB 361 and XPHB 391 remain separate source groups.
- 103 currently processed owners and 57 remaining convertible but unprocessed owners.
- 272 potential typed claims from all existing patterns in the offline assessment, up from 171.
- 0 `SUPPORTED`, 160 `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`, 428 `UNSUPPORTED` and 381 `NOT_ASSESSED`.
- 72 fewer saving-throw and 72 fewer damage structured-gap reason counts than the baseline; some owners moved to an explicit unsupported-pattern diagnostic without producing a claim.
- 39 owners gained a save claim and 44 owners gained a damage claim from the previously unmatched structured-gap group; these sets overlap.
- 0 per-Spell extraction failures in the final inventory run.

The 101 additional potential claims are from the full offline reassessment; the 19 added production claims are from the eight selected owners. These counts describe different scopes.

The inventory still cannot classify a Spell as `SUPPORTED`. Canonical `SpellMechanicsAssembly.finish` leaves `/entries` unassessed and marks emitted claim coverage `PARTIAL`. This coarse source-path coverage blocks full-scope classification even when the selected clauses are interpreted. This batch documents the limitation and does not redesign coverage tracking.

## Remaining gaps by observed impact

In the refreshed inventory, source-classified saving throws and damage still account for the largest unsupported groups: 272 save-family gaps and 252 damage-family gaps. Other observed gaps are condition (131), attack roll (42), spell-slot scaling (35) and damage resistance (33). These counts can overlap because one Spell may have several families.

Based on those corpus counts, the next extraction families with the greatest observed impact are condition, attack roll, spell-slot scaling, and damage resistance. This is a sequencing observation only; no work on those families is part of this batch.
